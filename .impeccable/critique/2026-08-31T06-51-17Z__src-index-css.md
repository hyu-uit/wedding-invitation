## What's Working

- **Stable family roles:** Computed styles consistently use Alex Brush for intimacy, Bodoni Moda for ceremony, and Noto Serif for factual copy on mobile and desktop.
- **Appropriate Vietnamese support:** Noto Serif is a sound choice for names, addresses, descriptions, and calendar details because it preserves diacritics and reads formally without feeling clinical.
- **Controlled display typography:** Bodoni Moda includes real 400 and 500 files, and those weights are used consistently for formal headings rather than synthesized by the browser.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Long Vietnamese verse is set entirely in Alex Brush

**Why it matters:** The two full lines in `.welcome-verse` are emotionally appropriate but harder to scan than short names or phrases, especially at the computed 24.8px mobile size. Dense joins and Vietnamese diacritics make the handwriting style fragile for guests with low vision or unfamiliarity with decorative scripts.

**Fix:** Set the verse in Noto Serif, optionally italic or centered with restrained tracking, and reserve Alex Brush for names, “We got married,” and the closing salutation. If the script must remain, increase the mobile size and line height, shorten the line measure, and test at 200% zoom.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Countdown requests a weight that does not exist

**Why it matters:** `src/App.css` assigns `font-weight: 700` to countdown numbers and labels, but the repository only declares Noto Serif 400. Because `font-synthesis: none` is set globally, there is no guaranteed bold face to render; results can become visually weaker or vary across browsers.

**Fix:** Add and declare a real Noto Serif 700 file, or deliberately use 400 and create emphasis through size, spacing, and contrast. A medium face can work if the visual intent is restrained rather than emphatic.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Later headings are scaled, not fully typeset

**Why it matters:** Bodoni headings such as `ADDRESS`, RSVP, and wish copy share the right family but rely heavily on large fluid sizes. At desktop, `ADDRESS` reaches about 76px while the closing script reaches 64px; the middle sections begin to feel mechanically enlarged and similarly weighted.

**Fix:** Define role-specific tokens for section title, utility label, emotional phrase, and closing signature. Tune line height, tracking, case, and maximum line length per role instead of using size as the primary hierarchy tool.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`

### [P3] First-load fallback can briefly change the invitation's voice

**Why it matters:** All faces use `font-display: swap`, so shared-link visitors on slower mobile connections can briefly see Times New Roman, Georgia, or a generic cursive. This is most noticeable in the hero names and may shift line breaks.

**Fix:** Preload only the critical above-the-fold Alex Brush and Bodoni files, convert fonts to WOFF2, and tune fallback metrics if layout shift remains measurable. Keep Noto Serif deferred unless body copy shifts materially.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable optimize`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent Guest):** The address body computes to about 11.2px on the tested mobile viewport, and the decorative verse compounds readability risk. **Casey (Distracted Mobile Guest):** There is no horizontal overflow, but the long folio and repeated ceremony/address/RSVP blocks make the essential date and location slower to reacquire after interruption. Several custom font and photo files also raise first-load sensitivity on slow mobile networks.
