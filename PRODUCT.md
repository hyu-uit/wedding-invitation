# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bridge and Bloom use the product to invite wedding guests through shared links. Guests open the link to view the invitation and understand the celebration details. In a later phase, guests will also be able to send wishes through a modal.

## Product Purpose

The product is a single-page digital wedding invitation that gives guests one convenient place to view the event, plan their attendance, and share in the celebration. Success means invited guests can quickly understand when and where the wedding takes place and use the relevant event actions without needing separate instructions.

## Operating Context

Bridge and Bloom distribute a direct link to invited guests. Guests are expected to visit primarily from personal mobile devices, with desktop access also supported. The current experience is view-only; interactive guest wishes are planned for a future phase.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present the couple's names, wedding date, venue, and event details.
- Provide a map or directions to the venue.
- Show a countdown to the wedding.
- Display a wedding photo gallery.
- Support RSVP, adding the event to a calendar, music, and invitation sharing.
- Add a guest-wish modal in a future phase.
- Remain usable as a shared-link, single-page web experience.
- The couple are Huyền Trân and Nhựt Hòa; the supplied wedding date is 16 September 2026 at 09:00.
- The invitation language is Vietnamese, with short decorative English labels where supplied by the approved references.
- Production deployment target and exact privacy behavior remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The working product identity is “Bridge and Bloom.” The invitation is for Huyền Trân and Nhựt Hòa. The supplied visual references commit the invitation to an ivory, black, and deep-red wedding palette; future surfaces should preserve that palette unless explicitly redesigned.

## Product Principles

- Make essential event information effortless to find on a phone.
- Keep the shared-link experience welcoming and simple for guests of varied technical comfort.
- Treat all names, dates, locations, photos, and private guest interactions as factual content that requires approval.
- Introduce interactive features progressively without compromising the invitation's core view-only experience.
- Give guests clear control over optional music, sharing, RSVP, calendar, and wish actions.

## Privacy

Privacy requirements are not yet defined. Before implementing RSVP or guest wishes, decide what personal data is collected, where it is stored, who can view it, and how guests are informed.
